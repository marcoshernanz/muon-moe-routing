# The third chain of the program (2026-09-26 to 28)

Scripts the assistant wrote to run the RUNBOOK's third chain (section 5: the confirmation tier, its rate re-check and partitions (a), (b), (c) at three seeds and 3B tokens, then the balancer block, (a) and (b) with the bias at its rate and four times faster on seeds 6 to 8, and both blocks' evaluation) on a rented Vast.ai host of four RTX 5090s, one worker per card, on the code of the tag `frozen-2026-09-26`, and to watch it from the laptop. They follow the second chain's (`../chain2_2026_09/`). The runs themselves come home to `code/runs/confirmation/` and `code/runs/balancer/` (the RUNBOOK's last section); this folder keeps the host's records, the laptop's watch log and the search for the host. The results are in `context/decision/EVIDENCE-LOG.md`, section 20.

Run the host's scripts from `~/code` on the host, `crosscheck.py`, `summary.py` and `exploratory.py` from `code/` on the laptop, and the laptop's other scripts from anywhere.

## What happened on the host

- The host of the chain: instance 52829819 from offer 51125578, California, host 557452 (the offset probe's), machine 148962, four RTX 5090s at a 600 W limit, driver 595.84, an EPYC 7B12 with 256 threads, 503 GB of memory, 250 GB of disk, 2.179 dollars an hour. Its image took 8 minutes to load.
- Setup: `uv sync` 299 s; the data 1,898 s (1,021 files, 19 GB), the Hub serving this host at about 12 MB/s, as it did the offset probe; the panel failed on the Hub's limit of 500 anonymous API calls in five minutes (`429 Too Many Requests` on the code slice's stream, after the data's calls), and built again once the window had turned, in 48 s, its five slices identical byte for byte to the laptop's of 2026-09-10.
- Checks: the step at the confirmation tier on the four cards at once, 746.4 and 742.7 ms under Muon and 655.9 and 661.9 under AdamW, against the cost model's 746.1 and 655.3, with peaks of 26.52 GiB allocated (29.53 reserved) under Muon and 28.28 (30.61) under AdamW, as RUNBOOK section 4 has them. The suite: 206 passed. The smoke, compiled: the re-check trained 9 runs (the 20-step runs put both winners at the top of their grids, AdamW's extension won and stayed an edge, and the block went on), then the 3 confirmation runs at 0.7 times the winners and the balancer block's 4, all evaluated on the panel and the annex, both blocks analysed (138 and 136 files), every run's settings as the plan fixes them, `O`, `O-router` and `U` read on the confirmation block and `S-fast`, `L` and `O-fast` on the balancer block. The suite left 16 GB under `/tmp/pytest-of-root`, deleted before the chain.
- The chain started on 2026-09-26 at 23:19:41 UTC, one worker per card, the four cards at 99 percent and 505 to 554 W on the re-check's first round.
- The confirmation tier's AdamW step settled at about 707 ms after the first 2,000 steps (671 before), against the step check's 656 to 662, at clocks of 2,840 to 2,916 MHz and 38 to 66 °C, so not heat: the chain runs about 7 to 8 percent slower than the cost model, about 38 hours with no rate at a grid's edge.
- The host's direct route to the laptop carried about 22 KB/s (a ping of 495 to 719 ms), while the same host uploaded to Cloudflare at 1.4 MB/s and Vast's ssh proxy carried about 2.9 MB/s, so `fetch.sh` runs through the proxy (`fetch.sh ssh9.vast.ai 29818`).
- The re-check's winners, AdamW 1e-3 and Muon 2e-3, both inside their grids, were written at about 05:47 UTC on 2026-09-27, and the 3B runs trained at 7e-4 and 1.4e-3. The balancer block started from about 00:40 UTC on 2026-09-28, on each card as its runs at 3B tokens ended, the last of which (the router on AdamW, seed 2) trained to about 10:40 UTC; card 2's worker exited cleanly at about 09:00 UTC with nothing left to claim.
- Training ended on 2026-09-28 at 10:42:48 UTC, one round, every worker exiting 0 and no run diverged: 35 h 23 min, 131.0 card-hours busy of 141.5. The evaluation took 8 min 22 s. The watch flagged no problem.
- From about 10:45 UTC Vast reported the instance offline and its proxy refused connections while the host ran on; the direct route then carried about 150 KB/s and the final fetch went that way, in 40 s: the 132 files of both blocks without their saves, identical by sha256 on the host and at home. The instance was destroyed at 10:52 UTC with no instance left, having spent 78.58 dollars.
- At home: `tfg.analysis` on both blocks and on the confirmation block against (b), `crosscheck.py` with no problem, `summary.py` and `exploratory.py`, and both blocks uploaded to the Hub's `marcoshernanz/tfg-runs` (542 files under `confirmation/` and `balancer/`, checked by downloading them back).
