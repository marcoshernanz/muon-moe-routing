# The third chain read again, and the scale probe (2026-09-28)

Scripts the assistant wrote after the third chain (`../chain3_2026_09/`) to read its logs for what no claim orders, and to test at the confirmation tier the reading that came out of them, on the thesis model, on a rented Vast.ai host of two RTX 5090s, on the code of the tag `frozen-2026-09-26`. Everything here is exploratory: it was looked for after the third chain's claims were decided, on the seeds that decided them. The results are in `context/decision/EVIDENCE-LOG.md`, section 21, and the literature checked against them in `context/decision/LITERATURE-MAP.md`, section G.

The question the logs leave: at the confirmation tier the first mixture's selection margins under the backbone on Muon fall to a few times the bias's step (under one step with the router on AdamW), the load churns, and the overload does not settle. The reading proposed: a direction every token's input shares gives the router's scores a large per-expert offset, the bias can cancel it only by bringing many tokens close to a tie between experts, and then each of its fixed steps moves many tokens at once. The probe asks whether taking the shared direction out of the first mixture's router input, the offset probe's intervention (`../rq3_reading_2026_09/offset_probe/`), removes the thin margins, the churn and the overload at the larger tier too, for the router on Muon and on AdamW.

Run `logs.py` and `analyze.py` from `code/` on the laptop, the host's scripts from `~/code` on the host, and the laptop's other scripts from anywhere.

## Scripts

| Script | Where | What it does | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| `logs.py` | laptop | Every routing log of the program (`code/runs/`): per arm and tier, the first mixture's logit norm, entropy, selection margins over the bias's step, churn, busiest expert with and without the bias, cosine, span and how often the busiest expert stays the same; the rank correlations over the runs of each tier; the confirmation tier per mixture; (c) − (b) per seed at both tiers. | `outputs/logs.txt` |
| `probe.py` | host | One run of the thesis model through imports of `tfg`, with readings added: per logged step and mixture the offset probe's per-expert readings and six numbers of the batch (the share of scores in the sigmoid's flat range above and below, the share of tokens one balancer step can move, the share of chosen experts the scores alone would not choose, the router's own margin without the bias, mean and 10 percent quantile), saved as `readings.npz`; with `--centre`, the first mixture's router scores each input minus the batch mean. | a run directory with the thesis logs, `readings.npz` and `probe.json` |
| `launch.py` | host | The probe's ten jobs in order of importance, one worker per card, each the third chain's confirmation-tier run of its arm and seed (compiled, K = 9, the 3B schedule, rates 7e-4 and 1.4e-3) stopped after step 3,000; no job starts after the deadline. | `logs/jobs.txt`, `logs/probe-*.txt`, `runs/scale-probe/` |
| `host.sh` | host | A guard that stops the instance at the cap whatever happens, the cards every minute, the environment, every shard of the data (so the data order is the chain's), then `launch.py`. | `logs/guard.txt`, `logs/gpus.csv`, `logs/setup.txt`, `logs/DONE` |
| `upload.sh` | laptop | The code of the tag (git archive) and the three host scripts into `~/code` on the host. | printed |
| `watch.sh` | laptop | The host's setup, jobs and cards every five minutes; exits on the end of setup, of a job, of everything, on an error in a job's log, an unreachable host or a heartbeat. | `outputs/watch.log` |
| `fetch.sh` | laptop | The finished run directories without their saves into `outputs/runs/`, the host's logs into `outputs/host-logs/`. | printed |
| `analyze.py` | laptop | Whether the plain runs reproduce the chain's logs to step 3,000; the first mixture per probe run beside the chain's runs of its seed; the probe's readings by window; the offset against the bias. | `outputs/analysis.txt` |
| `figure.py` | laptop | The draft of the plan's figure 13: every balanced run's first-mixture margin over the bias's step against its churn; the confirmation tier's margins per mixture and its overload over training; the probe's seed-0 runs, the busiest expert, the share of tokens within two bias steps of a switch and the share of choices the bias makes. | `outputs/knife-edge.png`, `.pdf` |

`outputs/instance.log` records the instance's creation, launch, destruction and spend; `outputs/literature-sweep.md` is the literature search's working notes.

All ten jobs ran on 2026-09-28 on one host of two RTX 5090s, 3 h 52 min for 4.29 dollars, and the instance was destroyed. The runs as run reproduce the chain's logs to step 3,000 bit for bit. Centring the first mixture's router input removes the thin margins, the churn, the overload and all of Muon's extra span over the first 3,000 steps, for the router on Muon and on AdamW at every seed, at the same loss. A bias four times faster removes most of the overload but not the churn. The numbers are in `EVIDENCE-LOG.md` section 21, items 3 to 5, and the runs are also in the private Hub dataset `marcoshernanz/tfg-runs` under `scale-probe/`.
