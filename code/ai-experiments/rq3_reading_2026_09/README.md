## The offset probe

The question the second chain's logs leave open is the step between the shared direction and the span. The router is linear, so the batch-mean logit of expert e is exactly `w_e · x̄`: the length of the batch-mean input (the shared direction, which the cosine reads) times the component of the router's row along it. The logs keep neither. The probe keeps both per expert and, as an intervention and not a proposal, removes the shared direction from the first mixture's router inputs alone.

It ran on the thesis model on 2026-09-26 (`EVIDENCE-LOG.md` section 19, item 12): 12 runs at the mechanism tier on a Vast.ai host of four RTX 5090s, 4.15 dollars, each the second chain's run of its arm and seed stopped after step 3,000; the plain runs reproduce the chain's bit for bit. Centring the first mixture's router input takes the early overload to AdamW's level and 79 percent of Muon's extra span with it, at no cost in loss; a bias four times faster takes most of the overload and 75 percent of the extra span, at no cost in loss; and the router on AdamW still overloads the first mixture at Muon's better rate (busiest expert 4.2 to 5.5 against 1.6 to 1.9) and still costs 0.0025 of loss at step 3,000. The shared direction's strength is three times AdamW's in every arm on the Muon backbone; the rows' lean along it spreads with the overload, and hardly at all with the faster bias. The rental is in `offset_probe/vast/outputs/instance.log`: instance 52771464 from offer 51174972 (host 557452), 120 GB of disk, destroyed after the fetch with no instance left. The runs, without saves, are ignored by git and kept in the private dataset `marcoshernanz/tfg-runs` under `offset-probe/` (uploaded and checked byte for byte on 2026-09-26).

A first version at the `kaggle_t4` tier (6 blocks, one dense and five mixtures of 16 experts, top 4, `d_model` 384, batch 16 at context 512, 3,000 steps with its own cosine) was pushed as the private Kaggle kernel `marcoshernanz/tfg-offset-probe-t4` on the Kaggle dataset `marcoshernanz/tfg-code` (the tag's code): at seeds 0 and 1, all Muon, all AdamW, all Muon centred, all Muon at half its rate, Muon on the experts alone, all AdamW centred; at seed 2, the first three. The mechanism-tier runs replaced it as the probe, and the kernel was stopped unfinished on 2026-09-26 on Marcos's request, with no outputs kept. A CPU check at the laptop tier matched the probe's offset to the mean logit to 2.4e-7, the centred mixture's mean logit read 1e-8 eager and 3e-8 compiled with the first mixture block recomputed, and the compiled run split no graph.
